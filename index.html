Trustworthy AI, from research to practice

I’m Prajit T. Rajendran, a Research Engineer at CEA LIST and a PhD graduate in Computer Science from Université Paris-Saclay. I build methods to detect AI failures, guide human oversight, and evaluate the safety of learning systems.

My work spans LLM safety, uncertainty estimation, and human-in-the-loop learning. At CEA LIST, I develop hallucination monitors for open-weight language models and evaluation pipelines for factuality and deceptive behaviour. My doctoral research focused on helping autonomous systems recognise what they do not understand and decide when to ask an expert.

I bring research and engineering experience across Python, PyTorch, Hugging Face, and GPU-cluster evaluation, alongside industry work at Upfluence, Scania, and MathWorks. I also collaborate on energy-efficient video streaming and quality assessment.
